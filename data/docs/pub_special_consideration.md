Special consideration is available when illness, injury or other serious circumstances outside your control affect your ability to complete an assessment or attend an exam.

Students must apply for special consideration through the student portal within three working days of the affected assessment due date or exam.

Applications must include supporting documentation, such as a medical certificate that covers the affected dates.

If an application is approved, the outcome may be an extension, a deferred exam or an alternative assessment. Approval is not automatic and is decided by the Student Central team with the subject coordinator.

Students can track the status of their application in the student portal. Most applications receive an outcome within five working days.
