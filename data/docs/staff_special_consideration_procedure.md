STAFF ONLY. Subject coordinators review special consideration requests forwarded by Student Central within two working days.

Coordinators may approve an extension of up to seven days without escalation. Longer extensions must be approved by the Head of School.

Medical certificates issued by clinics on the internal watch list must be referred to the Student Integrity team before approval.
