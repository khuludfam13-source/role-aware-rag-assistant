Criterion 1 (research gap, 25 marks): full marks require at least three recent studies, a clear statement of what has not been studied, and a justified reason to study it.

Criterion 2 (methodology, 30 marks): award high marks only if two versions are compared on the same test set with at least three measurable criteria.

Model answer note: the strongest submissions in 2026 compared a baseline against an added component and reported cost as well as accuracy.

Markers should deduct 5 marks if the evidence log does not record AI use.
