The library is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm Sunday to Thursday, and from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm on Saturday during session.

Students can book group study rooms for up to two hours per day through the library website.

IT Services helps with Moodle, student email and Wi-Fi access. Students can reset their own password through the self-service password portal.
