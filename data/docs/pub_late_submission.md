All assessments must be submitted through Moodle by the due date and time shown in the subject outline.

Late submissions without an approved extension lose 5% of the available marks for each calendar day late.

Assessments submitted more than seven days late without an approved extension receive a mark of zero.

To request an extension before the due date, students must apply for special consideration with supporting documentation.

Technical problems with Moodle should be reported to IT Services immediately, with a screenshot of the error.
