Students who believe an assessment mark is wrong should first discuss it with their tutor or subject coordinator within five working days of receiving the mark.

If the issue is not resolved, students can submit a formal request for review of a final grade within ten working days of the grade release date.

A review can raise, lower or keep the original grade.
