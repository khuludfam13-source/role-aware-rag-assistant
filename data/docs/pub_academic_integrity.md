Academic integrity means submitting work that is your own and acknowledging all sources you use.

Plagiarism, contract cheating, collusion and unauthorised use of generative AI tools are breaches of academic integrity.

Students may use generative AI only where the subject outline allows it, and must declare how it was used.

Suspected breaches are reported by the subject coordinator to the Academic Integrity Officer. Outcomes range from a warning to a zero mark or referral to the misconduct committee.
