STAFF ONLY. Final exam papers must be sent to the moderator at least four weeks before the exam period.

The moderator checks that questions match the learning outcomes and that the marking scheme is clear.

Final exam papers are uploaded to the secure exams drive and must not be stored on personal laptops.
