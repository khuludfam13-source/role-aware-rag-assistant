Students can add or drop subjects without penalty until the end of week two of the session.

Withdrawing from a subject after week two and before the end of week eight results in a grade of WD (withdrawn) and no academic penalty, but fees are not refunded.

Withdrawing after week eight results in a fail grade unless special circumstances are approved.

Enrolment changes are made through the student portal. Students on a visa should speak to Student Services before dropping below a full-time load.
